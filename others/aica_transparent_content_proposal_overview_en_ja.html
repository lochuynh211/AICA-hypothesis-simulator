AICA 透明コンテンツ提案アルゴリズム — Spotify限定 V1AICA transparent content-proposal algorithm — Spotify-only V1

一言で言うと：サービス選択が「音楽サービス」を選んだ後に、具体的な曲を選ぶアルゴリズムです。各曲を音声特徴からトレイト（活性度＝arousal／明るさ＝valence／歌いやすさ）に要約し、現在の状況が求める需要と一致する曲を高く評価します。すべて Spotify 互換メタデータと正確なID履歴だけで、既定で5曲の順序付きプランを出します。 In one line: after the service selector has chosen a music service, this picks the concrete songs. Each song is distilled from its audio into traits — energy (arousal), brightness (valence), and singability — and a song scores well when its traits answer what the current context demands. Everything uses only Spotify-compatible metadata and exact-ID history, returning an ordered five-song plan by default.

1概要 ― 考え方Overview — the idea

すべての採点特徴量は同じ形をとります：evidence e_i（現在の状況がどれだけ強く何かを求めているか）× response coefficient a_i（その曲がどれだけうまく応えるか）。新しいのは、6つのドライバー／環境特徴量で a_i をどう作るかだけです。 Every scored feature takes the same shape: an evidence value e_i (how strongly the context calls for something) times a response coefficient a_i (how well the song answers it). The only novelty is how a_i is built for the six driver/environment features.

なぜ2軸かWhy two axes

単一の「活性度」スカラーでは曲を「静か⇔激しい」でしか並べられません。静かで明るい曲と静かで暗い曲を区別できないのです。疲労・渋滞・夜間のドライバーは穏やかで前向きな曲を、眠気・単調路のドライバーは元気な曲を求めます。1軸ではこれらが同じ順位に潰れ、2軸なら分離できます。 A single “activation” scalar can only order songs calm↔energetic; it cannot tell calm-and-bright from calm-and-dark. A fatigued/congested/night driver wants calm and reassuring music, while a drowsy driver on a monotonous road wants energetic music. One axis collapses these; two axes separate them.

各レスポンス係数は |a_i| ≤ 1（音声特徴では |α_i| + β_i ≤ 1）。重みは非負で総和1。よって r_i ∈ [-1,+1]、Σ k_i ∈ [-1,+1]。item_fit の clamp は浮動小数の誤差対策のみです。 Each coefficient satisfies |a_i| ≤ 1 (for audio, because |α_i| + β_i ≤ 1); weights are non-negative and sum to one. So r_i ∈ [-1,+1] and Σ k_i ∈ [-1,+1]. The item_fit clamp guards only floating-point drift.

「実効重み」とは（3行で）What “effective weight” means (three lines)

base_weight_i = category_weight × subgroup_weight × leaf_weight
raw_weight_i  = base_weight_i × purpose_multiplier[purpose][subgroup] × mask_i
w_i         = raw_weight_i / Σ_j raw_weight_j

mask は Spotify限定V1 で採点可能なら1、そうでなければ0（採点不可の関係はトレースに残る）。最後の割り算だけが数字を不均一に見せる正体で、生き残った特徴量を総和1に再スケールしているだけです。mask is 1 if scorable in Spotify-only V1, else 0 (unsupported relations still appear in the trace). The final divide is the only thing that makes the numbers look uneven — it just rescales the survivors to sum to one.

3楽曲トレイト（4種）Song traits (four)

各曲を音声から4つのトレイト（各 [0,1]）に要約します。arousal と valence は感情の円環モデル（活性度×快さ）の2軸で、曲を1次元の音量線ではなく2次元の気分平面に配置します。歌いやすさ2種はカラオケサービス適合の判定にのみ使い、文脈判断には使いません。 Each song is distilled from its audio into four traits (each in [0,1]). Arousal and valence are the two axes of the circumplex model of affect (energy × pleasantness), placing a song on a 2-D mood plane instead of a 1-D loudness line. The two ease traits judge only a song's fit for a karaoke service, never context.

arousal — 活性度energy

曲がどれだけ元気か。ランキングで全サービス共通。How energetic the song is. Used in ranking, all services.

valence — 明るさbrightness

曲がどれだけ明るい／前向きか。全サービス共通。How bright/positive the song is. All services.

humming_ease — 鼻歌容易性hummability

口ずさみやすさ。鼻歌カラオケのみ。How easy to hum. Humming karaoke only.

full_karaoke_ease — 歌唱容易性singability

1曲通して歌いやすいか。フルカラオケのみ。How easy to sing the whole song. Full karaoke only.

フィールド別の根拠（抜粋）Per-field rationale (selected)

  • energy は強度そのもの→arousal の主項。loudness/tempo は強い相関、danceability は部分的、acousticness は弱い反転（アコースティック＝穏やか）。energy is intensity itself → arousal's main term; loudness/tempo strong correlates, danceability partial, acousticness a weak inverse (acoustic = calmer).
  • valence は快さの直接指標、mode（長調=明／短調=暗）は粗いが実在の手がかり。valence is the direct positiveness measure; mode (major bright / minor dark) is a coarse but real cue.
  • 歌いやすさは instrumentalness（反転＝ボーカルが必要）と speechiness（反転＝喋りは旋律にならない）が主。tempo は歌唱では中庸が最良（tempo_ease）。Singability is driven by instrumentalness (inverse — you need vocals) and speechiness (inverse — speech has no melody); tempo for singing prefers the middle (tempo_ease).
  • 保持するが未採点：key, time_signature, liveness。Retained but never scored: key, time_signature, liveness.

トレイト式Trait formulas

norm_loudness = clamp((loudness + 60) / 60)   norm_tempo = clamp((tempo − 60) / 120)
tempo_ease = 1 − clamp(|tempo − 110| / 90)   speech_ease = 1 − clamp((speechiness − 0.33)/0.33)
duration_ease = 1 − clamp((duration_ms − 180000)/180000)

arousal    = 0.30·energy + 0.25·norm_loudness + 0.25·norm_tempo + 0.15·danceability + 0.05·(1−acousticness)
valence    = 0.65·valence + 0.35·mode
humming    = 0.35·(1−instrumentalness) + 0.25·speech_ease + 0.20·danceability + 0.20·tempo_ease
full_kar   = 0.30·(1−instrumentalness) + 0.20·speech_ease + 0.20·tempo_ease + 0.15·duration_ease + 0.15·danceability

署名付きトレイト：A_s = 2·arousal − 1、V_s = 2·valence − 1。tempo は arousal では単調（速い＝活性）、歌唱では中庸最良（tempo_ease）と、同じフィールドを2通りに正規化します。Signed traits: A_s = 2·arousal − 1, V_s = 2·valence − 1. tempo is normalized two ways: monotonic for arousal (faster = more energetic) and centered for singability (tempo_ease).

4文脈レスポンス（2軸）Context response (two axes)

6つのドライバー／環境特徴量は、曲のフィールドを直接読むのではなく需要を表明します：arousal 需要 α と valence 需要 β。曲は署名付きトレイトで応え、状況の強さで掛け合わされます。 The six driver/environment features do not read song fields directly; they state a demand: α on arousal and β on valence. The song answers with its signed traits, scaled by how strongly the context is present.

feature_response  r_i(j) = e_i · ( α_i · A_s(j) + β_i · V_s(j) )
「符号は係数×トレイトに宿る」The sign lives in coefficient × trait

方向は evidence ではなく、係数の符号と曲トレイトの符号で決まります（evidence は常に強さ [0,1]）。例：疲労の係数は −0.50、穏やかな曲は −0.26 →「負×負＝正」で穏やかな曲を高評価。arousal は両側（活性⇔穏やか）、valence は片側（暗い曲は望まないので β ≥ 0）。 Direction comes from the coefficient sign × the trait sign, not from evidence (always a magnitude [0,1]). E.g. fatigue's coefficient is −0.50 and a calm song is −0.26 → negative×negative = positive, rewarding the calm song. Arousal is two-sided (active↔calm); valence is one-sided (β ≥ 0) — a driver context never wants dark music.

1つの表で足りる理由Why one table, not three

arousal/valence は曲の性質でサービスに依らないため3サービス共通。サービスで変わるのは Song singability 行だけ（鼻歌→humming_ease、カラオケ→full_karaoke_ease、プレイリストはmask0）。歌いにくい曲は鼻歌で減点されます。 Arousal/valence describe the song, not the service, so they're identical across all three. Only the Song singability row swaps source (humming→humming_ease, karaoke→full_karaoke_ease, playlist masked 0). A hard-to-hum song is penalized for humming.

8プラン生成と計算例The plan & worked example

plan = 適格曲をeligible songs sorted by ( item_fit desc, spotify_track.id asc ) → 先頭first plan_item_count (=5)

対比テスト（一部）Contrast tests (selected)

カタログ・サービス・設定・シードを固定し、1変数だけ変えて並べ替えを検証：低↔高 眠気/疲労/単調、通常↔渋滞、高速↔山道、昼↔夜（穏やか/活性の逆転）；子供在/不在（適格変化）；推しモード on/off；直近再生；直接利用度；回復率；走行↔停車（フルカラオケ適格）；route A↔B は順位変化なし（V1はルート-曲関係を作らない）。 Freeze catalog/service/config/seed, change one field, assert the reorder: low↔high drowsiness/fatigue/monotony, normal↔congested, highway↔mountain, day↔night (calm/active reversal); child absent↔present (eligibility); oshi mode on/off; recent play; direct usage; recovery; driving↔stopped (full-karaoke eligibility); and route A↔B = no rank change (V1 manufactures no route–song relation).

9保留事項と設計選択Pending & open choices

🔧 genre ベース特徴量Genre-based features

route・destination・child・hobbies・genre利用・scene/genre の6件。artist.genres 追加＋（Tier2は）ジャンル別履歴フィクスチャ＋対応表・応答係数の設計が必要。データ契約 v1.1 として別途仕様化。Six features (route, destination, child, hobbies, genre usage, scene/genre). Needs artist.genres + (Tier 2) per-genre history fixtures + affinity tables + response-coefficient design. To be specced as data-contract v1.1.

⚠ 疲労/渋滞/夜間 の方向Fatigue/traffic/night direction

本仕様は「和らげる（穏やか＋明るい）」方向を採用。代替の「覚醒維持（活性）」方向は該当3行の α 符号反転のみ。バージョン管理されたハイパーパラメータ。This spec adopts the “soothe (calm + bright)” direction; the alternative “keep-alert (active)” is just a sign flip on α for those three rows. A versioned hyperparameter.

⚠ 走行状態のランキングDriving-state ranking

走行→α·A_s（穏やか、暫定−0.30）、停車→0。眠気/単調と重複するため方向は製品判断待ち。適格ゲートとしての役割は不変。driving→α·A_s (calm, placeholder −0.30), stopped→0. Overlaps drowsiness/monotony, so the direction awaits a product call. Its eligibility-gate role is unchanged.

スケジュール（要検討）Schedule (open)

スライド71 はスケジュールを入力に挙げるが、曲との関係は緩い。artist.genres/Artist-ID があれば event→artist/genre 照合は部分的に可能。🔧 に昇格するか要判断。Slide 71 lists schedule as an input, but the song relation is loose. With artist.genres/Artist-ID, event→artist/genre matching is partially doable. Whether to promote it to 🔧 is an open call.
