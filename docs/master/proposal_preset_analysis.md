## Why cold-start scores looked "too low"

On a cold-start driver (no oshi, no history) the single best song in the whole catalog tops out near **0.10–0.20**: ~34% of the content weight (oshi / age / item-usage / played / acceptance / recovery) has nothing to act on, and by design that missing weight is never redistributed. The fix is **data, not a wider clamp** — a preset whose driver actually has an in-catalog oshi + play/recovery history activates that weight and legitimately reaches **0.37–0.43**. The retune above shifts weight toward preference/history so known-driver scores rise while cold-start stays honestly low.
